# The scout: art prompts (3D detective bot)

The film needs one character, generated by the owner in Google Flow or Higgsfield and animated by HyperFrames.
Make the **character sheet first**, then use it as the reference image for every pose and clip, so the bot looks the
same everywhere.

## How the art is used

- **Stills (PNG)**: each pose on a flat chroma-green background (#00B140). I cut them out
  (`npx hyperframes remove-background`) and move, scale and sequence them over the night map.
- **Short clips (MP4, optional)**: Higgsfield image-to-video from each still, 3-5 s, on the same flat green
  background, with a locked-off camera (no camera move). They give real walk and arm motion; I key them out the same
  way.
- Size: at least 2048 px on the long side. Full body in frame, feet visible, a little space around it.

## 1. Character sheet (do this first)

```
3D stylized character turnaround sheet of a small friendly detective robot, front view, three-quarter view,
side view and back view, standing in a neutral pose, full body, same scale in every view.

The robot: about the proportions of a 7-year-old child, a rounded rectangular head like a soft-cornered monitor
with a dark navy glass visor (#22374a) showing two simple vertical pill-shaped eyes in pale blue-grey (#c7d6e2),
no mouth. A belted tan trench coat (#a8865e) with the collar turned up and a brass buckle, a dark brown fedora
(#5a4330) with a black band, slim dark navy legs and small rounded boots. A small round badge on the left lapel:
a navy disc with a pale compass needle. A slate-blue canvas satchel backpack (#2c4f68) with a flap and a brass
clasp, a rolled pale blanket on top. It holds an old brass lantern in its right hand.

Style: soft matte 3D, like a premium stop-motion figure: clay and felt and brushed-metal materials with visible
fabric texture, soft studio key light from the upper left, gentle contact shadow on the ground, no rim glow,
no bloom, no neon, no lens flare, no sparkles, no particles, no text, no logo except the lapel badge.
Flat chroma-green background #00B140, evenly lit, nothing else in the frame.
```

## 2. Poses (each with the character sheet as the reference image)

Add this line to every pose prompt: *Same character as the reference sheet, same materials and colours, three-quarter
view facing right, full body, flat chroma-green background #00B140, soft key light from the upper left, no glow,
no text.*

| File name | Pose prompt |
| --- | --- |
| `scout-idle.png` | Standing still, holding the unlit brass lantern down at its side, looking slightly up and to the right, curious. |
| `scout-lantern-on.png` | Same as idle, but the lantern is lit with a small warm flame (#e7a95a), the light falling softly on its coat. No glow halo. |
| `scout-magnifier.png` | Holding a brass magnifying glass up to its visor with the left hand, leaning forward, the lantern lit in the right hand. |
| `scout-walk-1.png` / `scout-walk-2.png` | Mid-stride walking to the right, left foot forward in one, right foot forward in the other, lantern lit, coat swinging. |
| `scout-reading.png` | Head tilted up as if reading a sign above it, the lantern lifted to shoulder height. |
| `scout-packing.png` | Turned slightly away, satchel swung to the front with its flap open, putting a small wooden name plate inside. |
| `scout-handoff.png` | Facing the camera, holding the closed satchel out towards the viewer with both hands, proud. |
| `scout-tip-hat.png` | Facing three-quarter left, touching the brim of its fedora with one hand, the lantern in the other. |

## 3. Clips (Higgsfield, optional, image-to-video from the stills)

Locked-off camera, no zoom, no camera shake, plain green background throughout, 3-5 s each:

- `scout-walk.mp4` (from walk-1): walks steadily to the right across the frame, coat and lantern swinging.
- `scout-search.mp4` (from magnifier): slowly sweeps the magnifying glass from left to right, head following.
- `scout-pack.mp4` (from packing): drops the name plate into the satchel and closes the flap.
- `scout-handoff.mp4` (from handoff): lifts the satchel towards the camera.
- `scout-tip-hat.mp4` (from tip-hat): tips the hat once and nods.

## Where to put them

Save to `video/explainer/assets/scout/` with the file names above. Until the files arrive, the preview uses the
code-drawn stand-in rig (`assets/scout.js`) in the same poses, so the timing can be approved first.
