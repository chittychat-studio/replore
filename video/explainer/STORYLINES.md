### The mix: "11:40 pm, then the scout"

1. **Hook (0-4 s):** laptop clock 11:40 pm; her chat box reads "build me a chatbot that answers questions from my PDFs";
   the AI replies "Sure! Let's start from scratch…"
2. **Tension (4-12 s):** from-scratch code piles up, an error lands, she sighs. (A)
3. **Reveal (12-16 s):** she opens Replore and types the same sentence; as she presses Find, the camera dives into the
   search box and the screen becomes the scout's topographic map at night. (A to C)
4. **The scout searches (16-34 s):** the lantern flicks on; the sentence is the signpost; spyglass scan ("Searching
   GitHub"); three points light up ("Ranking by fit"); the scout walks to each: reads its licence sign ("Free for
   commercial use"), peeks inside (Wiki), packs two into the backpack ("Add to plan"). (C)
5. **Payoff (34-44 s):** the scout hands the backpack across the map's edge, and we are back on her screen: three real
   cards (kotaemon, pdfGPT, pdftochat) and the plan opening in Claude, which now builds on kotaemon. (back to A)
6. **Claim + end card (44-52 s):** "Find it before you build it." The compass mark is carried in; replore.cardzo.co.in.

Look: her desk in flat illustration, then the banner's topographic map in brand navy/slate with the ASCII globe as the
night sky; site fonts. Sound: lo-fi bed, keys, an error thud; in the scout world, footsteps, lantern click, a warm theme,
a zip on "Add to plan". The scout: a code-drawn flat character for the first preview, swappable for Flow/Higgsfield art.

The one thing to remember: **before you ask an AI to build something, Replore finds the open-source repo that already does most of it, and tells you if you can use it.**
Spine (all three): one real idea, *"a chatbot that answers questions from my PDFs"*, worked through to Replore's real top results for it (Cinnamon/kotaemon, bhaskatripathi/pdfGPT, Nutlope/pdftochat; all MIT or Apache-2.0, scout.py on 2026-10-23).

Assumptions for all three: 45-60 s, 16:9 (a 9:16 cut can follow), no voiceover, large on-screen lines carry the words, free music from the HyperFrames registry. A HeyGen voice or avatar is optional and paid; the free option is no voice.

## A. "11:40 pm" (recommended: a person a stranger recognises, and the pain before the relief)

**Device:** a moment in someone's day, then life before and after. **Form:** one continuous take across a desk.

A vibe coder at 11:40 pm asks her AI to "build a chatbot over my PDFs". The AI starts writing everything from scratch: the code scroll grows, an error appears, she sighs. The camera pushes past her chat window into a browser tab: she types the same sentence into Replore, the ASCII globe spins, and three real repos land with "Free for commercial use". She taps **Wiki** to understand kotaemon, adds two to her plan, and the plan opens in Claude: the AI now builds on proven code.

- **First 2 s:** a laptop clock reads 11:40 pm; a chat box holds "build me a chatbot that answers questions from my PDFs" and the reply begins "Sure! Let's start from scratch…"
- **Beats:** hook (11:40, the ask) → tension (from-scratch code piles up, an error) → reveal (the same sentence moves into Replore) → proof (globe scans · "Ranking by fit" · three cards land · Wiki · Add to plan ×2) → payoff (plan opens in Claude, building on kotaemon) → claim "Find it before you build it." → end card (compass mark carried in, replore.cardzo.co.in).
- **Look:** a real desk at night that turns into the site's own navy and slate; the site's fonts (Anton, Inter Tight, JetBrains Mono); the ASCII globe as the one signature material.
- **Sound:** low lo-fi bed; keyboard clicks; a soft error thud; a bright chime when the cards land.

## B. "One sentence's journey" (the clearest "how it works")

**Device:** the worked example; the object is the explanation. **Form:** object-to-object chain, every shot ends on the thing that opens the next.

The sentence itself is the hero. It splits into the search words Replore really generates ("chatbot pdfs python", "rag chatbot", "chat with pdf"), which dive into the ASCII globe of GitHub. Repos stream out and pass through real filters one gate at a time (open-source licence, updated in the last 24 months, enough stars), then line up ranked by fit. The top three get their licence verdict stamped, and two drop into a plan that becomes a prompt for Claude.

- **First 2 s:** the sentence types itself huge, centred, then its words break apart into three search chips.
- **Beats:** hook (sentence → search words) → into the globe → filters as gates → ranked by fit → licence stamp → add to plan → prompt → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the site's navy, slate and mist; mono labels on every gate; the ASCII globe; no people.
- **Sound:** a clean pulse bed; a tick per gate; a stamp sound on the licence.

## C. "The scout" (the bold one)

**Device:** a character does the work. **Form:** a flight through one world.

A small scout with a lantern walks a topographic map made of repos. The visitor's sentence is written on a signpost; the scout raises a spyglass, the map lights up in three places, and it walks to each one: checks the licence sign ("Free for commercial use"), peeks inside (Wiki), and packs two into its backpack (the plan). At the end it hands the backpack to an AI builder.

- **First 2 s:** the lantern flicks on in the dark, lighting a signpost that reads "a chatbot that answers questions from my PDFs".
- **Beats:** lantern + signpost → spyglass scan → three lights → licence sign → peek inside → backpack → hand-off → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the banner's topographic map; flat brand colours; the scout drawn from a Flow/Higgsfield character sheet (art needed first).
- **Sound:** footsteps, a lantern click, a warm theme; a pack-zip on "Add to plan".
- **Needs from you:** the scout character art. Without it this one waits.
